Checkpoint: theme ideas

Checkpoint / Design High priority #design #themes Edited 2h ago

Direction

Midnight is the base. Deep ink backgrounds, one violet accent, and mint for anything live. Every other theme keeps the same layout and only swaps the palette.

Contrast rule
Accents never carry white text. Use the theme's ink color on top instead.
Aa Atkinson Hyperlegible A typeface built for readability, by the Braille Institute. fonts.google.com
